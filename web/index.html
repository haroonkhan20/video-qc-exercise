Detailed settings — optional

You can leave these as they are. Changes here let you fine-tune the choices above.

How much each area matters

A larger percentage gives an area more influence on the final score. Together they must add up to 100%.

When to use each score label

Lower scores are below the standard. Serious file or data problems always prevent approval and limit the Overall Score to 2 or 4, depending on the issue. Missing information is never treated as a perfect result.

How much variation is allowed

All times below are in seconds. A smaller allowance is more demanding.

How much of the recording has a problem

For a 100-second recording, 1% means 1 second. Set the mark for each amount of affected time or readings. Overlapping problems are counted once. No affected time gets 10.

How far a measurement exceeds its allowance

For example, a 1-second delay is twice a 0.5-second allowance. Choose the mark for each level. Anything above the last level gets 0.
