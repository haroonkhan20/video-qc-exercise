RECORDING EVIDENCE

Create your grading standard

Save a new profile; existing standards and original QC reports are preserved. Adjust scoring and acceptance, not the original image or gap detectors.

Category weights

Weights must total 100%. A category uses the mean of required streams, each controlled by its worst applicable parameter.

Grade recommendations

Below review is unacceptable. Critical defects stay unacceptable and cap the grade at 4. Unknown required checks stay not assessed, even at zero weight.

Grading limits

Affected time or reading fraction

Overlapping runs count once. A positive affected fraction uses the first matching upper boundary; zero earns 10. More severe fractions cannot earn higher scores.

Exceeding a grading limit

Within the limit earns 10. Above the last multiplier earns 0. For example, an end lag of 1 second divided by a 0.5-second limit is 2 times the allowance.
